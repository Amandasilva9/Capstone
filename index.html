What we measure

Joint angles and posture markers from your webcam, shown in plain language.

Knee extension

How straight each leg gets when you stand. A fully straight knee reads close to 180°.

Hip extension

How upright your hips sit from the side, which can show tightness at the front of the hip.

Ankle angle

The angle between shin and foot, shown for reference alongside your other measurements.

Shoulder and hip level

Whether your shoulders and hips sit level when seen from the front and back.

Knee alignment

How closely hip, knee and ankle line up, which helps spot knees that drift inward or outward.

Left vs right

Side-by-side comparison so you can see where one side moves differently from the other.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any special equipment?

No. You need a laptop or computer with a webcam, enough space to stand 6 to 8 feet back, and a well-lit room.

Is my video recorded or uploaded?

No. The video is processed in your browser. Only the measured angles are sent to your account so you can see your history.

How accurate are the measurements?

They are good for noticing differences and tracking change over time, but a single webcam is only an estimate and can be off by several degrees. It is not a clinical measurement.

Is this a replacement for physical therapy?

No. It is not a rehabilitation tool. If you have pain or an injury, talk to a healthcare professional before exercising.

What should I wear?

Fitted clothing helps the camera see your joints. Avoid baggy clothes and make sure your whole body, head to feet, is in view.
