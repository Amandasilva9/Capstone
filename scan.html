Ready for your scan?

Four quick views: front, left side, right side and back. It takes about two minutes.

  • Place your laptop on a stable surface so your whole body fits in view, head to feet.
  • Stand about 6 to 8 feet back, in a well-lit room with a plain background.
  • Wear fitted clothing so your joints are easy to see.
  • You get at least 8 seconds before each photo to get into position.
Your video stays on this device. Only the measured angles are sent for analysis.
